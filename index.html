The Elder Scrolls: Arena

An unofficial fan remake in Three.js. Bring your own copy of the game.

This remake ships none of Bethesda's files. Choose the ARENA folder from your copy of the game. In the free Steam release it sits inside the game's install folder, and it holds GLOBAL.BSA. Your files are read in this browser and are never uploaded.
