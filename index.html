The Elder Scrolls: Arena

An unofficial fan remake in Three.js. Bring your own copy of the game.

This remake ships none of Bethesda's files. Choose the Arena folder from your own copy (the one that contains GLOBAL.BSA). Arena is free from Bethesda if you need it. Your files are read in this browser only and are never uploaded.
